## Description (two lines)

Mailchat Vault turns Gmail Takeout, mbox, and WhatsApp/Telegram/Signal/Discord/Messenger exports into one private, searchable archive on your own machine.
Search stays on this device — fully offline, free, and open source (GPL-3.0-only).

The second line's privacy wording comes from the canonical positioning
block ([`docs/landing.md`](../../landing.md)); do not reword it in one
place only.
